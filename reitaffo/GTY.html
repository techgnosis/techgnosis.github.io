  • Green: a new all-time high, higher than every earlier quarter. The first quarter on record is green too, since it is the best so far.
  • Payout: the dividend as a percent of AFFO. Green here marks a new all-time low.
  • Dividend: the common dividend per share declared for the quarter. Green: only when the cents go up, so a sub-cent raise isn't highlighted. Red: lower than the previous quarter's dividend.
  • +2.1% in 3q under a high: how far it rose above the previous high, and how many calendar quarters that took. Gains use the actual figures, even for dividends. The first high after a restart has none.
  • Grey: a quarter inflated by a one-time item the company left in its AFFO, such as a legal settlement or back rent. It doesn't count toward highs.
  • highs restart: the business fundamentally reset here. This quarter is the new starting point, so as the first quarter of the new run it is highlighted as a high (AFFO, dividend) and a low (payout). Later quarters are compared with it, and the quarters below the line no longer count.
  • Notes: what the one-time item was in a one-off quarter, or why the business reset at a restart.

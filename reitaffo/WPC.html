Shown in full; it is green only when the cents go up, so a sub-cent raise isn't highlighted. Its source is the AFFO release when that states the dividend, otherwise the company's own dividend announcement (linked as "announcement").
  • Grey: a quarter inflated by a one-time item. It doesn't count toward highs.
  • highs restart: the business fundamentally reset here. This quarter is the new starting point and is never highlighted itself. Later quarters are compared with it, and the quarters below the line no longer count.

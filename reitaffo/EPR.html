  • Payout: the dividend as a percent of AFFO. Green here marks a new all-time low. One-off quarters and $0 dividends don't count.
  • Dividend: the common dividend per share declared for the quarter, from the same release as the AFFO figure. Shown in full; it is green only when the cents go up, so a sub-cent raise isn't highlighted. Its source is the AFFO release when that states the dividend, otherwise the company's own dividend announcement (linked as "announcement").
  • Grey: a quarter inflated by a one-time item the company left in its AFFO, such as a legal settlement or back rent. It doesn't count toward highs.
  • highs restart: the business fundamentally reset here. This quarter is the new starting point, so as the first quarter of the new run it is highlighted as a high (AFFO, dividend) and a low (payout). Later quarters are compared with it, and the quarters below the line no longer count.
